Install it

  1. Open this page on your Android phoneand tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the downloaded fileIf Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap InstallThen open Neja Music.

Updates

Neja Music checks this page for new versions. On Wi-Fi it downloads them by itself and shows Update ready; tap Install and Android updates the app in place. Your playlists, likes and downloads stay.
